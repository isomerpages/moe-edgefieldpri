<h2>Achievements</h2>
<h4><strong>Celebrating Our Young Mathematicians!</strong></h4>
<p></p>
<p>Our school is incredibly proud to recognize the outstanding dedication
of our Primary 5 and Primary 6 students who recently stepped up to represent
us on the big stage!</p>
<p></p>
<p>Selected students put their problem-solving skills to the test in two
of the most prestigious math competitions in the region:
<br>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>The Asia Pacific Mathematical Olympiad for Primary Schools (APMOPS), organized
by Hwa Chong Institution.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The National Mathematical Olympiad of Singapore (NMOS), organized by NUS
High School of Math and Science.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p>These competitions are known for pushing the boundaries of mathematical
thinking, and our students met the challenge head-on. Beyond the complex
equations and logical puzzles, we want to celebrate the resilient efforts
of our young thinkers.
<br>
