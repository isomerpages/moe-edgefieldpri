<h3>EDGE Digital Literacy Programme</h3>
<p>Empowering Students to Think Critically, Act Responsibly, and Create Confidently
in the Digital Age.
<br>
</p>
<h3>Our Purpose</h3>
<p>The EDGE Digital Literacy Programme equips students with essential digital
skills, computational thinking competencies and responsible technology-use
habits. Through progressive, hands-on learning experiences from Primary
1 to Primary 6, students learn to create with technology, solve problems
collaboratively and communicate effectively in an increasingly digital
world.</p>
<h3>Our Learning Approach</h3>
<p>The DL Programme is anchored in experiential, reflective, and values-based
learning:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Reflective Practice: Students reflect on their growth and decisions across
the module.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Real-World Application: Projects mirror authentic digital tasks like poster
design or video production.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Values-Driven Use: Students are guided to use digital tools with empathy,
respect, and integrity.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Progressive Design: Skills and complexity deepen from foundational awareness
(P1) to creation and communication (P6).</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Primary 1 and Primary 2: ScratchJr Block-Based Coding</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/DL_Pic_1.jpg">
</div>
<p><em>Bringing ideas to life! A student explores block-based coding on ScratchJr, building creativity, computational thinking and problem-solving skills—one block at a time.</em>
</p>
<p>Students are introduced to basic coding concepts through ScratchJr, a
visual block-based programming platform. Working individually and collaboratively,
they create a digital artefact related to environmental sustainability.</p>
<p>Through the programme, students learn to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Create step-by-step instructions to solve simple problems.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Break down problems into smaller, manageable parts.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Recognise patterns and apply basic algorithmic thinking.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use sequences, loops and repetitions to animate characters and objects.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Identify and correct simple coding errors through trial and improvement.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Collaborate effectively in pairs or groups.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Primary 3 and Primary 4: Google Workspace Learning</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/DL_Pic_2.png">
</div>
<p></p>
<p>Students develop foundational digital productivity and communication skills
using Google Docs and Google Slides. They learn to organise information,
create purposeful digital content and present their ideas clearly to an
intended audience.</p>
<p>Through the programme, students learn to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Create, edit and format documents and presentations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Organise and present information clearly and effectively.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Incorporate text, images and other media appropriately.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Demonstrate effective online search skills.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Practise safe, responsible and ethical use of online information.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Collaborate with peers using shared digital tools.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Communicate respectfully and provide constructive feedback.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Primary 5: mBlock Coding and AI Fundamentals</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/DL_Pic_3_jpg.jpg">
</div>
<p><em>Coding meets AI! Our Primary 5 student brings the classic Scissors, Paper, Stone game to life using mBlock’s AI features.</em>
</p>
<p>Students build upon their computational thinking skills through mBlock
coding and guided, age-appropriate vibe-coding experiences. They design
a digital artefact linked to environmental sustainability while exploring
fundamental concepts in Artificial Intelligence.</p>
<p>Through the programme, students learn to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Decompose problems, recognise patterns and plan algorithms.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Apply programming concepts such as loops, conditionals and variables.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Debug, test and refine their solutions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develop organised, modular and readable coding habits.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Understand what AI is, how it learns and some of its common applications.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Demonstrate effective search skills and practise safe and ethical online
behaviour.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use appropriate digital communication tools to share information with
an intended audience.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Collaborate effectively in pairs or groups.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>Our Impact</h3>
<p><strong>Developing Future-Ready Competencies</strong>
</p>
<p>Across all levels, students are encouraged to demonstrate Creative, Adaptive
and Inventive Thinking (CAIT) throughout the learning process. They are
given opportunities to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Explain their reasoning and reflect on their learning.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Consider different perspectives and adopt varied strategies.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Generate, modify, evaluate and refine their ideas.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Collaborate effectively towards shared goals.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Communicate respectfully while exchanging ideas and co-constructing knowledge.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Through these progressive learning experiences, the EDGE Digital Literacy
Programme nurtures confident, responsible and creative users of technology
who are prepared to thrive in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.</p>
